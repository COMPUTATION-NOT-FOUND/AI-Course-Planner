How it works

  1. 01

    Block your busy hours

    Click the hours you can't attend: a job, a commute, Friday prayers. Any section meeting then is left out.

  2. 02

    Pick what you want, or nothing

    Lock in sections you've decided on and shortlist electives. Courses you leave open are filled with whichever section fits.

  3. 03

    Compare and export

    Browse the ranked timetables, filter by days on campus, and add the one you like to your calendar.

Under the hood each required course is a variable whose values are its open sections, and Google's OR-Tools CP-SAT solver enumerates the assignments with no two meetings overlapping. If nothing complete fits, it falls back to the timetables that drop the fewest courses and tells you which ones are missing.
